PRIVACY POLICY

The shortest honest privacy policy in Minecraft-land: we barely store anything, and it all stays yours.

1. WHAT WE DO NOT DO

  • No accounts, no logins, no sign-up walls for visitors.
  • No analytics, no trackers, no ad networks, no fingerprinting.
  • No selling or sharing of data — there is no data to sell.
  • No cookies are set for tracking purposes.

2. WHAT IS STORED, AND WHERE

  • Browser localStorage (your device only): favorites, submissions, settings, API-cache entries. Nothing from this storage is transmitted anywhere.
  • sessionStorage (browser session only): admin unlock flag for the Publish dashboard.
  • Server side: the CurseForge proxy on Cloudflare forwards your search keywords and filters to the official api.curseforge.com to fetch results, exactly as the official API requires. No personal identifiers, no profiles.
  • Third-party APIs: Modrinth (api.modrinth.com), CurseForge (api.curseforge.com) and Mojang (piston-meta.mojang.com) receive standard requests for public data. Their own policies apply to their services.

3. YOUR UPLOADS & CONTACT

  • Upload submissions live in your browser until an admin approves them on their device. If a server-sync backend is configured, submissions are transmitted to that server for review.
  • Contact messages sent via email go to us only, and we use them to reply. Nothing else.
  • You can wipe everything instantly in Settings → "Reset site data".

4. CHILDREN & CONTACT

Minecraft is for everyone — this site collects nothing from anyone, including children. Questions? Use the Contact page. Policy updated: .
